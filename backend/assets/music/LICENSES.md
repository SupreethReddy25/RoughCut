# Bundled music

Every track in this folder was synthesised from scratch by `backend/scripts/make_music.py` (sine / triangle / filtered-saw oscillators and noise percussion). They contain no samples and no third-party material, so they are original works dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `sunrise.mp3`: Sunrise (calm, 80 bpm)
- `lofi_drift.mp3`: Lo-fi Drift (chill, 76 bpm)
- `bright_steps.mp3`: Bright Steps (upbeat, 112 bpm)
- `neon_pulse.mp3`: Neon Pulse (energetic, 120 bpm)
- `quiet_hours.mp3`: Quiet Hours (mellow, 66 bpm)
- `coffee_break.mp3`: Coffee Break (light, 92 bpm)
- `rise_up.mp3`: Rise Up (motivating, 100 bpm)
- `night_drive.mp3`: Night Drive (moody, 104 bpm)
